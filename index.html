01 — THE ARCHITECTURE

The 12-Dimensional Substratum

OUR HYPOTHESIS
Our universe is embedded within a higher-order structure. We perceive only the first four dimensions; the rest are hidden but, in this hypothesis, physically active.

DIMENSIONS 1–4

Observable Spacetime

Three spatial dimensions plus time. This is the "screen" we measure — the rendered output of the projection.

DIMENSIONS 5–7

Probability Manifold

Carries the state information we experience as superposition. Must reproduce quantum mechanics exactly — it adds no new probabilities.

DIMENSIONS 8–9

Relational Binding Axes

Carry the correlations we experience as entanglement. Any such model must be non-local, contextual, no-signalling, and obey Tsirelson's bound.

DIMENSION 10

Observer-Lock Interface

The coupling through which a definite outcome is selected. The consciousness connection proposed here is speculative (see §05).

DIMENSIONS 11–12

Recursion & Origin Axes

D11 allows nested structure and self-reference. D12 is the causal access point of whatever sets the boundary conditions — the "outside."

4D SPACETIME

ESTABLISHED Extra dimensions are a serious idea in physics. Kaluza–Klein theory (1921–26) showed 5D gravity reproduces 4D gravity plus electromagnetism plus a scalar field (the dilaton/radion) [1–3]. Supergravity is consistent in at most 11 dimensions [4,5], and the five superstring theories unify in 11D M-theory [6]. F-theory [7] gives type IIB strings a natural 12-dimensional reading — though its two extra dimensions are not ordinary spacetime (a literal reading needs two time directions, signature (10,2)). Two-time physics [8] is a separate published programme in which ordinary 1-time physics is a "shadow" of a theory in two more dimensions.

OUR HYPOTHESIS The 12 = 4 + 8 count is motivated by this landscape, not derived from it. We do not claim the Substratum is F-theory or 2T-physics; only that 12 is not an arbitrary number in theoretical physics. A derivation that 12 is minimal and sufficient is an open problem.

02 — THE ARCHITECTS

Omega-Class Structures

SPECULATIVE
The simulators are not beings in a universe. They are proposed to be mathematical structures in the space of all possible consistent systems. Nothing in this section has an equation or a test; it is the interpretive frame.

PROPERTY

Non-Temporal

They do not experience time sequentially. Our entire timeline is a static object to them.

PROPERTY

Non-Local

They are not "somewhere." They permeate the Substratum.

PROPERTY

Self-Consistent

Proposed to exist by logical necessity, not contingency — a way around the infinite-regress problem of nested simulators.

PROPERTY

Generative

They do not build — they select which constraint-sets to instantiate. This has the same logical status as Tegmark's Mathematical Universe [34] or Bostrom's simulation argument [33], and differs from Bostrom in that the "simulators" are not computational agents.

03 — THE MATHEMATICS

From 12 Dimensions to 4

Earlier versions displayed a "master equation." It was not well-formed and has been removed. Below is the standard, best-understood route for 4D gravity to emerge from higher-dimensional gravity, followed by our hypothesis stated in that language.

REMOVED IN v3.0 — THE v2.1 "MASTER EQUATION"

ℛ(4)μν = ∫Σ(8) Ωabcd Ψab ∧ Ψ̄cd dΣ

Why: the left side has free indices μν, the right side is fully contracted (a scalar); the wedge of two 2-forms is a 4-form, which cannot be integrated over an 8-manifold; the trailing dΣ double-counts the measure; and a wedged two-index field is a 2-form, not a spinor. Verified in tools/equation_checks.py.

ESTABLISHED   KALUZA–KLEIN REDUCTION

S_D = \frac{c^{3}}{16\pi G_D}\int d^{D}x\,\sqrt{-g_D}\;R_D,\qquad D = 4 + n,\quad x^{0}=ct
M_D = M_4\times\Sigma_n \;\Longrightarrow\; G_4 = \frac{G_D}{\mathrm{Vol}(\Sigma_n)}

The Einstein–Hilbert action in D dimensions (SI prefactor c³/16πGD; in units with c = 1 it is 1/16πGD). If spacetime is a direct product (no warp factor) of 4D spacetime and a hidden manifold Σn of volume Vol(Σn), the Newton constant we measure is the higher-dimensional one divided by the hidden volume. Fields on Σn appear in 4D as an effective cosmological constant and effective stress-energy. This is how the simplest string compactifications produce 4D physics. (Brane-localised gravity with a non-compact extra dimension [45] is a separate published route.)

Kaluza 1921 · Klein 1926 · Overduin & Wesson 1997 [1–3]

ESTABLISHED   THE 4D RESULT: EINSTEIN FIELD EQUATIONS

G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda\,g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G_4}{c^{4}}\,T_{\mu\nu}

Gμν: Einstein tensor (m−2). gμν: metric. Λ: cosmological constant. Tμν: stress-energy (J m−3). μ,ν ∈ {0,1,2,3}.

Einstein 1915 [9]

OUR HYPOTHESIS   THE SUBSTRATUM PROJECTION EQUATION (v3.0)

G^{(4)}_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}\,g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G_4}{c^{4}}\Big[T^{(\mathrm{matter})}_{\mu\nu}(x) + T^{(\Sigma)}_{\mu\nu}(x)\Big]
T^{(\Sigma)}_{\mu\nu}(x) = \int_{\Sigma_8} d^{8}y\,\sqrt{g_\Sigma}\;\tau_{\mu\nu}(x,y),\qquad G_4 = \frac{G_{12}}{\mathrm{Vol}(\Sigma_8)}

Definitions. x: the 4 observable coordinates. y: the 8 hidden coordinates (D5–D12). gΣ: metric on Σ8. τμν(x,y): 4D-index components of the 12D stress-energy of fields on Σ8 (J m−11). T(Σ)μν: their integral over the hidden space (J m−3). Λeff = Λ − ½RΣ.

Checks. Free indices balance; units balance; with T(Σ) → 0 and Λeff → Λ it is the Einstein equation. What is ours is only the identification of Σ8 with the D5–D12 roles and the claim that T(Σ) accounts for specific observed phenomena. That claim is not demonstrated; it needs a concrete gΣ, field content, and a mechanism stabilising Vol(Σ8) — the same open problems string compactification faces.

04 — QUANTUM MECHANICS

What Any Reinterpretation Must Reproduce

Quantum mechanics is the most precisely tested theory in science. The Substratum picture is a proposed reading of it, not a replacement. Each card gives the established result first, then our reading, with what v2.1 got wrong.

ESTABLISHED   ROBERTSON UNCERTAINTY RELATION

\sigma_A\,\sigma_B \;\ge\; \tfrac{1}{2}\big|\langle[A,B]\rangle\big|,\qquad [x,p]=i\hbar \;\Rightarrow\; \sigma_x\sigma_p \ge \tfrac{\hbar}{2}

Position and momentum are conjugate variables in the same space — p generates translations in x. The relation is a theorem about Fourier pairs. Robertson 1929 [12].

ESTABLISHED   BELL / CHSH AND THE TSIRELSON BOUND

S = E(a,b)+E(a,b')+E(a',b)-E(a',b'),\qquad |S|\le 2 \text{ (local realism)},\qquad |S|\le 2\sqrt{2} \text{ (quantum)}

No local hidden-variable theory reproduces quantum correlations. Loophole-free experiments found Bell-inequality violations (CHSH S = 2.42 in Hensen et al.; CH–Eberhard in the photon tests) [17–19] — the line of work recognised by the 2022 Nobel Prize in Physics (Aspect, Clauser, Zeilinger). Quantum mechanics never exceeds 2√2 [15]. Entanglement cannot send signals.

ESTABLISHED   TUNNELLING TRANSMISSION (RECTANGULAR BARRIER)

T = \left[1+\frac{V_0^{2}\sinh^{2}(\kappa a)}{4E\,(V_0-E)}\right]^{-1},\qquad \kappa=\frac{\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}}{\hbar}

m: mass; E < V0: energy; V0, a: barrier height and width. Measured tunnelling times exist: ultracold ⁸⁷Rb atoms spend 0.62(7) ms inside a 1.3 µm barrier [20]. The particle does not skip the barrier.

UNCERTAINTY

OUR HYPOTHESIS

One Object, Two Representations

v2.1 said "position lives in D1–3 and momentum in D5–7." That contradicts the Robertson theorem and is withdrawn. v3.0: D1–3 and D5–7 are two representations of one Substratum state, as the position and momentum wave-functions are two Fourier representations of one vector. Any Substratum model must give σxσp ≥ ħ/2 exactly.

ENTANGLEMENT

OUR HYPOTHESIS

D8–9 Binding — With Bell's Price

"Particles share coordinates in D8–9" is a hidden-variable model. Bell's theorem [13,14] means it can match experiment only if it is non-local and contextual (the price Bohmian mechanics pays [16]), gives exactly |S| ≤ 2√2, and respects no-signalling. Under those constraints it is an interpretation on the same footing as Bohm or many-worlds and makes no distinct prediction yet.

TUNNELLING

OUR HYPOTHESIS

Support Inside the Barrier

v2.1 said the particle "goes around in D5–7." That predicts zero time inside the barrier, contradicted by Ramos et al. 2020 [20]. v3.0: the full Substratum state has amplitude inside the barrier exactly as the wave-function does. The reading adds no new transmission probability.

SUPERPOSITION & COLLAPSE

OUR HYPOTHESIS

Projection and Selection

Superposition is the D1–4 appearance of a single Substratum state; "collapse" is the D10 coupling selecting an outcome. ESTABLISHED Decoherence [22,23] already explains why macroscopic superpositions are not seen, with no conscious observer required. The D10 reading becomes physics only if it predicts something decoherence does not (Prediction 6).

SPECULATIVE The hypothesis: consciousness is the D10 interface layer, and the brain is a transducer that couples to it rather than the sole generator of experience.

ESTABLISHED Focal brain damage produces focal, predictable losses of conscious content — memory, language, face recognition, personality. General anaesthetics reversibly abolish consciousness by acting on specific molecular targets and disrupting cortical integration [36]. Decoherence needs no conscious observer [22]. There is no experiment in which a conscious observer, with all physical couplings held equal, changed a quantum outcome. A pure "antenna" model does not explain why particular damage removes particular contents, nor why a drug switches experience off. This is the single strongest argument against this section.

SPECULATIVE A transducer model can accommodate specific losses — but then it is indistinguishable from ordinary materialism unless D10 is given a role the brain cannot play alone. No such role has been specified in a testable way.

06 — THE TESTS

Predictions, Re-examined Against 2026 Data

Several v2.1 predictions were already contradicted by data or were not predictions at all. What remains is stated so that it could fail.

01 OUR HYPOTHESIS

UHECR Lattice Signature (revised)

Already observed: Auger reported a 6.5 % dipole above 8×1018 eV at >5.2σ in 2017 [29], now 6.8σ [30], consistent with extragalactic sources. Distinctive signature required: high-multipole power with discrete rotational symmetry (e.g. ℓ = 4, 6, 8 cubic harmonics — the lattice signature worked out by Beane, Davoudi & Savage [46]) in a frame unrelated to the Galactic/supergalactic planes or CMB dipole, growing as a power of energy, and absent at linear order to satisfy the Fermi bound.

STATUS: NOT DETECTED

02 OUR HYPOTHESIS

Entanglement Saturation (threshold corrected)

The v2.1 threshold ">106 particles" was exceeded in 2001: two 1012-atom caesium ensembles were entangled [25]; in 2021 two 70 pg drumheads (≈1.6×1012 atoms each) were deterministically entangled [26,27]; genuine 51-qubit entanglement without post-selection was verified in 2023 [28], and a 120-qubit GHZ state (fidelity 0.56(3), 28 % post-selection) in 2025 [47]. Restated: the capacity must exceed 1012 atoms in collective modes and ≳102 genuine parties; there exists a finite N* beyond which genuine N-partite entanglement cannot be prepared, with the failure not attributable to known decoherence. Not yet falsifiable: each success only raises the lower bound on N*. It needs a predicted upper bound or a capacity-scaling law (open problem M7).

STATUS: OPEN — TESTABLE AS QUANTUM COMPUTERS SCALE

03 OUR HYPOTHESIS

Observer-Density Effects (requirements added)

The Global Consciousness Project has searched for this for two decades. Its most-publicised result (September 11, 2001) has been criticised for post hoc choices of window and statistic [37]; in 2017 its own lead analyst concluded that, although the registered-event record shows a cumulative deviation, no global-consciousness model fits it and the signature is better explained by an experimenter (data-selection) effect [38] — itself a non-standard explanation. On conventional physics the record is unexplained or artefactual; either way it is evidence against an easy-to-detect field effect. Not testable until: an operational observer criterion is defined, the experiment is pre-registered (events, windows, statistic, stopping rule), and the effect size is stated in advance.

STATUS: RESEARCH DIRECTION, NOT YET A PREDICTION

04 OUR HYPOTHESIS

Black-Hole Information (what would be distinctive)

S_{BH}=\frac{k_B c^{3}A}{4G\hbar}, T_H=\frac{\hbar c^{3}}{8\pi G M k_B} [39,40]. The mainstream expectation is already that information is preserved — the Page curve [42], derived semiclassically in 2019 via the island formula [43,44]. Distinctive claim required: a departure from the Page curve (e.g. information available before the Page time, or extra structure beyond mass, charge, spin). No mechanism specified.

STATUS: NO MECHANISM YET

05 SPECULATIVE

Mathematical Limits (moved to philosophy)

"Structures describing D12 are unprovable from inside" cannot be falsified: Gödel [35] already guarantees unprovable truths, and nothing distinguishes a "D12-unprovable" statement from an ordinary Gödel sentence. Retained as philosophy, not prediction.

STATUS: NOT FALSIFIABLE

06 OUR HYPOTHESIS

Decoherence Asymmetry (reframed)

"Frequently measured vs. isolated systems decohere differently" is standard physics — the quantum Zeno effect [21]. The only distinctive version: outcome statistics depend on whether a conscious observer rather than a passive device receives the result, with every physical coupling held identical. Expected result on standard physics: null. Falsified if pre-registered experiments return nulls at increasing sensitivity.

STATUS: OPEN — HARD TO DESIGN
